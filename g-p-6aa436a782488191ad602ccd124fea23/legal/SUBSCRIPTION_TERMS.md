Features may vary by platform and version. A subscription does not guarantee matches, responses, rehearsals, work, income, or visibility to any specific person.

## Price and renewal

The price, currency, billing period, taxes, and any trial or introductory offer are displayed before confirmation. Unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period or as otherwise stated by the billing provider, the subscription automatically renews and the provider charges the payment method associated with your account.

## Platform billing

- **iOS:** Apple processes purchases. Manage or cancel through Apple account subscription settings. Apple’s billing and refund rules apply.
- **Web:** Stripe may process purchases. Manage through the Delos billing portal made available on the web account.

Purchases generally do not transfer between unrelated store accounts. Restoration requires the same Apple account or other provider identity used for purchase.

Except where required by law or provider policy, charges are nonrefundable and no prorated credits are provided for unused time, downgrades, inactivity, suspension for misconduct, or feature changes.
