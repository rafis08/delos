# Delos Privacy Policy (beta draft)

Delos collects account credentials through Supabase, musician profile information you provide, approximate location for distance matching, uploaded media, likes, matches, messages, session proposals, reports, blocks, device notification tokens, and subscription status. Exact addresses and private email addresses are not displayed to members.

We use this information to operate discovery, matching, messaging, safety, notifications, and subscriptions. We do not sell personal information. Access is restricted through database row-level security. You may delete your account from Settings; this removes the associated profile data and media.

Before public release, replace this draft with counsel-reviewed terms containing the operating company, contact address, jurisdiction-specific rights, retention periods, subprocessors, and an effective date.
