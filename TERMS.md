# Delos Terms and Community Standards (beta draft)

Delos is for adults 18 and older. Members must own or have permission to share uploaded media, respect other members’ boundaries, avoid harassment, impersonation, hate speech, scams, and illegal content, and use safe public locations when meeting. Delos may review reports, restrict accounts, and remove content to protect the community.

Before public release, replace this draft with counsel-reviewed terms covering ownership, licenses, acceptable use, disclaimers, dispute terms, governing law, and contact details.
