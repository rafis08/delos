# Delos brand system

## Brand idea

**Where music is born.**

Delos is where musical potential becomes a real room, a real rehearsal, and a real band. The identity connects the island of Delos—mythic birthplace of Apollo—with the practical optimism of finding the right collaborators.

## The guardian

The Naxian lion is the primary symbol. It represents guardianship, permanence, courage, and Delos itself. Use it as a confident signature, never as decoration scattered across every screen.

### Brand hierarchy

1. **Hero mark:** Large guardian with the wordmark and slogan. Reserve for welcome, launch, major campaigns, and premium brand moments.
2. **Primary lockup:** Guardian plus DELOS. Use for sign-in and other major entry points.
3. **Compact lockup:** Small guardian, DELOS, and optional slogan. Use once in persistent product areas such as Discovery.
4. **Product mode:** No repeated logo. Let typography, gold actions, warm surfaces, and direct copy carry recognition.

## Color

- Marble white `#FFFFFF`: primary canvas and the guardian's material.
- Apollo gold `#FFC53D`: primary action and brand signal.
- Solar orange `#FF7A1A`: energy, artwork, and expressive moments.
- Espresso `#1B140A`: primary type and outline.
- Warm ivory `#FFF9EF`: cards and quiet surfaces.

Gold should indicate brand or action—not become a general-purpose fill for every component. Red remains reserved for destructive and safety actions.

## Voice

Delos is assured, inviting, and musically literate. It avoids startup jargon, forced mythology, and exaggerated promises. Headlines can be expressive; interface instructions should be direct.

Preferred language: “Start discovering,” “Make it real,” “Plan a session.”

Avoid: mythological puns, “rockstar,” “revolutionary,” and calling every interaction “epic.”

## Guardrails

- Keep the slogan exactly lowercase: **where music is born**.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolor, or place the guardian over a visually noisy photograph.
- Preserve clear space around the mark equal to roughly one quarter of its height.
- Do not place the full lockup more than once on a screen.
- Keep musician imagery dominant inside discovery and profile experiences.
