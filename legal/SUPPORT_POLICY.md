## What to include

Provide the email associated with the account, a concise description, relevant dates, usernames or content links, and screenshots when appropriate. Never send your password, full payment-card number, government ID, or security codes through ordinary email.

## Safety and moderation

Use the in-app report and block tools when possible. We prioritize credible threats, stalking, sexual exploitation, content involving minors, nonconsensual intimate media, and fraud. Delos is not an emergency service; contact local emergency services for immediate danger.

Users may appeal account or content actions at `[APPEALS EMAIL]`. Include the account email, decision being appealed, and why it should be reconsidered. Submission does not guarantee reversal, and we may decline repetitive, abusive, or fraudulent appeals.

## Response expectations

We aim to acknowledge ordinary support inquiries within five business days and urgent safety reports as soon as reasonably practicable. These targets are not guarantees. Complex, abusive, incomplete, or legally restricted matters may require more time.

## App Store subscription support

Apple controls charges and refunds for iOS subscriptions. Delos can help identify entitlement status but cannot issue an Apple refund. Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription.
