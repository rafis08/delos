# Delos launch legal checklist — internal

This file is operational guidance and should not be published as a policy.

## Required before submission

- [ ] Obtain legal review of all public documents.
- [ ] Replace every bracketed placeholder.
- [ ] Publish each public document over HTTPS.
- [ ] Link Privacy, Terms, Community Guidelines, and Support inside Delos.
- [ ] Add the Privacy Policy URL and accurate privacy disclosures in App Store Connect.
- [ ] Ensure the public policy matches the actual production vendor configuration.
- [ ] Confirm AIFIXMY LLC remains active and in good standing in New Jersey.
- [ ] Use an address approved for public legal notices; do not expose a home address unintentionally.
- [ ] Create monitored role-based email addresses and an incident-response process.
- [ ] Register and maintain a DMCA designated agent with the U.S. Copyright Office.
- [ ] Confirm account deletion deletes user content and warns about active Apple subscriptions.
- [ ] Test report, block, appeal, media removal, notification consent, and deletion flows.
- [ ] Establish moderation response standards, escalation rules, evidence preservation, and law-enforcement handling.
- [ ] Complete vendor agreements and data-protection terms for enabled processors.
- [ ] Confirm appropriate business, cyber, media, and general-liability insurance with a broker.
- [ ] Document retention and deletion jobs so published timeframes are operationally accurate.
- [ ] Preserve affirmative acceptance records for Terms and material updates.
- [ ] Present subscription price, billing period, renewal, cancellation, and restore-purchase information before purchase.
- [ ] Keep Stripe checkout absent from the iOS purchase path.
- [ ] Prepare a written security and data-breach response plan.
- [ ] Review state registrations, taxes, and consumer obligations with a CPA or attorney as operations expand.

## Recommended user contracts outside platform terms

Delos should educate users—but not automatically become a party—to separate musician agreements covering songwriting splits, master ownership, band name and trademarks, credits, expenses, gear, confidentiality, performance compensation, departure rights, and dispute resolution.

## Release record

For each policy version, retain the final text, effective date, publication URL, approval, app version, and evidence of how users accepted or were notified.
